What changed in v2

① Nested groups

A repeat group can now contain other groups, to any depth. Here Inverters (repeat right ×4) lives inside Block (repeat down ×3) — so each of the 3 stacked blocks gets its own row of 4 inverters. Uncommon, but it makes complex arrays buildable.

② Create by grouping, then drag in

⟳ group on a row starts a group around it. Then drag other rows — or whole groups — onto the group (header or its drop zone) to pull them inside. ungroup dissolves a group, lifting its contents back out one level.

③ 2-axis replication (down & right)

Every group — and the top-level Repeat whole sequence — has two independent counts: ↓ down (stacks copies as more rows) and → right (tiles copies along the row direction), each with its own gap. Set either to 1 to disable that axis; set both to tile a 2-D grid.

Physical-layout preview

The schematic shows the actual 2-D result (batteries blue, inverters orange) so nesting + tiling read at a glance instead of as a flat list.

Data model (recursive): item = row | group, where group{ repeatDown, gapDownM, repeatRight, gapRightM, gapToNextM, items:item[] }. The top-level sequence is an implicit outermost group. Today's “repeat sequence ×N” = that group's repeatDown. Materialiser becomes a recursive block layout: lay out a group's items into a block, then tile the block down×right.
