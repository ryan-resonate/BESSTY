---
title: Barriers
section: Building a model
---

Click **+ Barrier**, then click to drop each wall vertex. **Double-click** or press **Enter** to finish. A wall can be a single straight segment or a multi-vertex polyline that bends around a site. Each barrier carries a top height in metres above local ground, edited inline in the Barriers tab.

The solver applies `Abar` per ISO 9613-2 section 7.4 along every source to receiver path the wall intersects, combining the per-band `Dz` with `Agr`.

While drawing: **Backspace** removes the last vertex, **Esc** cancels. After placing a wall, select it to drag any vertex or the whole line.
