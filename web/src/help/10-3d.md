---
title: 3D view
section: Reference
---

Open the 3D dialog from the floating map controls. Sources and receivers render at their actual height above the local DEM — WTGs as thin lines from ground to hub, BESS and auxiliary units as cuboids, receivers as small spheres.

The vertical exaggeration slider scales both the terrain mesh and the object heights uniformly, so 1x is true scale and 5x makes hub-versus-receiver differences obvious. The camera tilts from top-down to 85 degrees, about 5 degrees above the horizon.
