---
title: Calculation area
section: Building a model
---

The yellow dashed rectangle on the map. Drag the centre handle to move it; drag a corner handle to resize. Numeric inputs in the Area tab always reflect the current geometry.

Grid spacing auto-adapts on first creation (about 200 cells per side) and locks against your choice once you touch the picker. A larger area takes longer to evaluate; the cell count shows beneath the spacing dropdown.
