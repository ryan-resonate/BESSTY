---
title: Receivers
section: Building a model
---

Click **+ Receiver**, then click on the map. Each receiver has a name (click to edit), a height above ground, and three period limits: day, evening and night. Limits are read in the project's assessment weighting (dB(A) by default — see Settings), and the field labels follow it. The active period is the one used to colour the pass/fail badge.

Selected receivers group-drag with sources to reposition layouts. Multi-select with shift-click or a left-button box drag.

Turn on **Show limits on markers** in the Layers tab to display the active limit under each level.
